# Privacy Policy

**App name:** Card Games Calculator  
**Effective date:** November 11, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Card Games Calculator** (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the App on Android or other supported platforms.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. About the App

Card Games Calculator is a local point-calculator app for card games, including:

- Callbreak  
- Call Kitty  
- Marriage etc

The App helps you enter player names, bids/tricks or round scores, and view calculated results. It is designed to work primarily **on your device**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

When you use the App, you may enter:

- Player names  
- Game settings (for example, number of rounds or players)  
- Scores, bids, tricks, “seen” status, and related match data  

This information is used only to run the calculators and show scoreboards.

### 2.2 App preferences stored on your device

The App may store locally:

- Theme preference (light / dark)  
- In-progress or recent match / session data so you can continue a game  

### 2.3 Information we do **not** collect

We do **not** intentionally collect or upload:

- Personal contact details (email, phone number, address)  
- Photos, contacts, or microphone / camera data  
- Payment or financial information  
- Advertising IDs for ad targeting (the App does not show third-party ads at this time)  

---

## 3. How We Use Information

Information entered or stored in the App is used to:

- Calculate and display game scores  
- Remember your theme and active match where applicable  
- Improve your experience within the App on your own device  

We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** use your game data for advertising.

---

## 4. Where Data Is Stored

Game data and preferences are stored **locally on your device** using on-device storage (for example, local preferences and a local database for Marriage sessions).

We do **not** operate a cloud account system for this App in the current version, and we do **not** send your player names or scores to our own servers.

If you uninstall the App or clear the App’s storage, this locally stored data may be permanently deleted and may not be recoverable.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties.

Because the App stores data on your device, standard device backups (for example, system backups you enable) may include App data according to your device / OS settings. That is controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

The App is a general utility for card-game scoring and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in the App, please contact us so we can help you remove local data by clearing App storage or uninstalling the App.

---

## 7. Permissions

The current version of the App is designed to work without requesting sensitive permissions such as location, camera, contacts, or storage access beyond normal app-private storage.

If future versions request additional permissions, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, explain the purpose in the App or store listing.

---

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information stored by the App on your device by keeping data in the App’s private storage. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for physical access to your device and for any lock screen / device security you enable.

---

## 9. Your Choices and Controls

You can:

- Edit or clear player names and scores inside the App (where the feature allows)  
- Reset or start a new match  
- Clear App data from your device settings  
- Uninstall the App to remove App-associated local data
